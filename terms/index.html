It replaces the standard licence agreement that used to sit behind the "Terms of Use" link, because FFF now lets you post photos and text where other players can see them, and that needs rules written in plain words rather than borrowed ones. Read the short version if you read nothing else. By tapping "I agree" in the app, or by using FFF at all, you accept these terms.

The short version

  • There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour in FFF.
  • Post only photos you took or own, of things you have the right to show. Nothing sexual, nothing hateful, nothing violent, nothing illegal, and no photographs of other people who have not agreed to be posted.
  • Anyone can report any photo, any post and any player, from the item itself, in two taps. Every report is reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.
  • Anyone can block any player instantly, without reporting them and without telling them.
  • Content that breaks these rules is removed. Accounts that keep breaking them are ejected from the shared map, permanently, without a refund from us.
  • Your photos stay yours. You give FFF only the permission it needs to show them inside the app.
  • Nothing in FFF rewards speed. Do not touch the phone while you are driving.

Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

FFF has zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. There is no warning system for the serious cases, no strike count to run down and no appeal that keeps the content up while it runs. If you post something that breaks the rules below, it is removed. If you use FFF to harass, threaten, impersonate or endanger another person, your access to the shared map ends.

This applies to everything a player writes or uploads: display names, handles, vehicle nicknames, profile photos, vehicle photos, feed posts and anything attached to them.

What you may not post

Do not upload, post or send any of the following. This list is not a technicality. It is the thing this app will be judged on.

  • Sexual content, nudity, or anything sexualised. There is no artistic exception and no "it was a joke" exception.
  • Any image of a child in a sexual or exploitative context. This is reported to the authorities, not just deleted, and the account is ended permanently and immediately.
  • Hate speech, or attacks on a person or group because of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, age, sexual orientation or gender identity.
  • Harassment, threats, stalking, doxxing, or posting anyone's home address, workplace, phone number, licence plate or anything else that helps a stranger find them.
  • Graphic violence, gore, self harm, or the encouragement of any of it.
  • Photographs of other people who have not agreed to appear in FFF. If a face is recognisable and the person has not said yes, do not post it. Children's faces are never acceptable, including your own children.
  • Photographs you did not take and do not have the rights to, including images pulled from the web, from a manufacturer's press kit, from a dealer listing or from another player's profile.
  • Anything illegal where you are, anything promoting illegal drugs or weapons, and anything advertising a scam, a giveaway or a service.
  • Photographs or posts that celebrate dangerous driving. Speedometers, drag races, street racing, riding without a helmet, filming while driving. FFF does not rank speed and it will not host content that glorifies it.
  • Impersonating another player, a real person, a company or the developer.
  • Spam, bulk posting, automated posting, or content that has nothing to do with the game.
  • Cheating, faked GPS, modified builds, or any attempt to claim territory you did not physically cover.

Photos are checked automatically before they appear to anyone, and they can be checked again by a human at any time afterwards. A photo that fails the automatic check is never published. Passing the automatic check is not approval, and it does not stop a photo being removed later.

Reporting, and the 24 hour commitment

Every photo, every post, every comment and every player name in FFF carries a report control. On a photo it is the "..." button in the top right corner of the image. On a player it is inside the player sheet, which opens when you tap their name anywhere in the app.

Every report is reviewed and acted on within 24 hours of being received. Reported content is hidden from the person who reported it the moment they report it, so no one has to keep looking at something while it is being dealt with. Where the report is upheld, the content is removed and the account behind it is warned, suspended or ended, depending on what it did. Where a report involves a child, it is escalated immediately and reported to the relevant authorities.

You can also email fff.turf.app@gmail.com about anything in the app, including content you found upsetting, content you believe infringes your copyright, and photographs of yourself that you did not agree to. Say what it is and where you saw it and it will be handled on the same 24 hour clock.

Reports made in bad faith, in volume, or as a way of attacking another player are themselves a breach of these terms.

Blocking

Open any player's sheet, tap Block, and they are gone. Their territory colour, their handle, their profile, their photos and their posts stop appearing for you, on the map, in the feed and on the leaderboard, at once. They are not told. You can unblock from Settings. Blocking is free for everybody, forever, and it is not part of any subscription.

Your photos stay yours

You keep every right you already had in the photos and words you upload. You are not selling them to FFF and FFF does not claim them.

What you do give is the narrow permission needed to run the app: a non exclusive, worldwide, royalty free licence to store your content, to re-encode and resize it, and to display it inside FFF to the players you posted it to, for as long as you keep it posted. That licence ends when you delete the content or your account, except for copies already held in backups, which age out, and copies retained for a safety investigation, which are covered in the Privacy Policy.

Two things follow from that. Your uploads are stripped of their embedded metadata on the server before anyone sees them, including the GPS coordinates a phone writes into a photo, so posting a picture of your car outside your house does not publish your address. And nothing you post is used to train anything, sold to anyone, or shown outside FFF.

You are responsible for having the right to post what you post. If you upload someone else's photograph, that is on you, not on FFF.

Age

FFF is for players aged 13 and over, or over the age of digital consent where you live, which is 16 in some European countries, unless a parent or guardian agrees on your behalf. If you are under that age, do not use FFF. Accounts found to belong to children under 13 are deleted.

The game, and the road

FFF measures ground covered. It does not measure speed, it does not rank speed and it never will. No hexagon is worth a collision.

Start your activity before you set off. Put the phone in a mount or a pocket. Do not touch it, do not read the feed and do not take photographs while you are driving or riding. Obey every traffic law and every local rule about where you can walk, ride and drive. You are responsible for how you move. FFF is a game layered on real streets, and the streets do not care that you are playing.

Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms, not by us. An account ended for breaking these terms is not refunded by us, and losing access to FFF PRO is part of what being ejected means.

Ending it

You can leave at any time. Settings, then "Delete my account and data". Everything on the server and on your phone goes, your territory is released, and there is no login, so nothing can be restored afterwards.

We can end your access, without notice, if you break these terms. Serious breaches, meaning anything in the child safety, sexual content, hate or threat categories, end an account permanently on the first occurrence. Creating a new account to get around an ending is itself a breach.
