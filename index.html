GPS Territory Game

Your city is a map full of empty hexagons

FFF is a territory game played on the real streets around you: move through a hexagon and it turns your colour, and everything after that is other people trying to take it off you.

How the map changes hands

Three rules. That is the whole game.

01

Claim it by moving

Press GO and every hexagon your route touches gets claimed in your name, on foot, on a bike or on the road. Each one is about 0.1 square kilometres. There is nothing to tap. You just have to actually be there.

02

Rivals steal it back

Cross ground someone else holds and it flips to you. Their phone buzzes with your handle on it: two hexes gone, come and take them back. Most of them do. That is where the game actually lives.

03

The Rust takes what you neglect

A hexagon lives seven days. Pass back through your own ground and it holds for longer. Forget about it and The Rust gets it: grey ruins owned by nobody, free for anyone to reclaim. You get one warning the night before.

Nothing you claim stays yours

One shared map, an area leaderboard ranked on ground held, and a record of everything you have ever taken. Go and claim the boring hexagons near your door while nobody is paying attention.
